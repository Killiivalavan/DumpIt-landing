Okay, serious for a sec.

You’re about to type your messiest thoughts into an app from a stranger. Fair to ask.

Who can read my dumps?
Your entries are tied to your account and protected by access controls. We don't use your journal entries for advertising.
Where do they live?
Your entries are saved on your device and synced to our cloud backend so you can access them across devices.
Can I delete everything?
Yes — request account deletion here. Confirm via email and your account and all entries are gone within three calendar days.
Read the full privacy policy →  Sign-in is currently Google only.
iPhone person?
There’s no App Store version yet. Leave your email and I’ll ping you when there is. One email, no newsletter.

Questions, probably.

Is it free?
Yes. DumpIt is free with no paid plans.
Why an APK and not the Play Store?
DumpIt is distributed directly for now. A Play Store release may come later.
Will it update itself?
Not yet. For now, grab the latest APK from this page whenever a new version drops. Once DumpIt is on the Play Store, updates will be automatic.
Do I need a Google account?
Yep. Google sign-in is how you get into DumpIt and sync your entries.
